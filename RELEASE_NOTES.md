## Included

- New name and logo throughout the app, About, seven languages, tray identity, Windows executable/installer/shortcuts and exports.
- Quick and Full study modes; focus, recall, reflection/log and rest; floating digital/circular timers; Library, History, Stats and Learn.
- Recall/log Save for later, retained drafts, History browsing state, saved-note pagination and standalone local-note deletion.
- Active-session History protection and rejection of overlapping time edits.
- Accessible Settings labels and keyboard focus recovery; seven-language support including Arabic RTL.
- Mixed-subject analytics attribution fixes and clear limits on what self-ratings and study time establish.
- Existing profiles, backups, appearance presets and Obsidian markers/attachments remain compatible. No user data migration or reset is required for the rebrand.

## Updating

Back up data in Settings, back up exported notes/vaults separately, and quit the old app completely through its tray icon before running Setup. Keep the same installation scope and destination. Do not delete profiles or databases. The internal version **0.1.6-beta.1** is newer than the previous 0.1.5-beta.2 build; the public release name is **Beta 1** for the A14 Pomodoro brand.

## Verification and limitations

Passed: **1,200 tests in 90 files**, both typechecks, production build, seven-language copy checks, matching-source rebuild (16 outputs), all **119 installer payload files**, embedded icon comparisons and production security checks. Earlier isolated branded UI checks covered light/dark themes, narrow windows, collapsed navigation and seven-language About screens. Repository screenshots use fictional records and retain their capture manifests; they were not relabeled as new captures for this version-only release step.

**Not performed:** actual clean-install or in-place upgrade/uninstall/reinstall, physical sleep/monitor/DPI endurance, native screen-reader certification or a learning-effectiveness study. Payload extraction does not execute Setup. The installer is unsigned. Windows may display unknown-publisher/SmartScreen warnings; keep protections enabled and report threat detections. No independent trademark clearance or full recursive Chromium rebuild is claimed.

This release ships **Windows x64 only**. Linux/macOS remain on their older experimental **FocusRecall** builds. Earlier release tags and file/checksum names remain historical records; they were not renamed or overwritten.
